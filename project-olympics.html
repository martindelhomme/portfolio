01 - Context

A personal initiative for a world-class fencer

Auriane Mallo-Breton and I trained at the same club before she joined the INSEP, France's national high-performance sports institute. A high-level fencer throughout her career and a regular member of the French national team, she was one of the top contenders heading into the Paris 2024 Olympics.

This project was entirely my initiative. No one asked for it. Coming from both a data background and years of competitive fencing experience, I saw an opportunity to apply structured analysis to a domain where almost none exists: opponent scouting in elite fencing.

"Fencing has almost no public data infrastructure. No shot charts, no tracking, no stats feeds. Building a scouting system meant starting from scratch - defining the framework, finding the data, and processing it manually."
02 - Approach

Building the methodology from zero

The scope: analyse all 35 qualified opponents for the Women's Epee individual event at Paris 2024 - the 35 best fencers in the world. The goal was to give Auriane and her coach structured data and video support they could use in preparation.

The data pipeline involved three sources: the FIE (International Fencing Federation) website for official rankings and results, Fencing Worldwide for detailed round-by-round match data, and official competition live streams on YouTube for video footage.

01
Define the scope and time window
9 international competitions over the last 18 months. Beyond that, results distort the picture of current form. Only World Cup or Grand Prix level competitions counted.
02
Classify every opponent by profile
The FIE only records handedness. Grip type (pistol vs. French) is not tracked publicly anywhere. Each opponent's grip had to be determined manually through video review.
03
Build individual result files
For each opponent: a dedicated Excel file tracking every round of every competition - opponent faced, score, win/loss, and the profile of the opponent they faced.
04
Filter for relevant matchups
Since Auriane is left-handed with a pistol grip (GC), the key data was how each opponent performed against GC fencers - the rarest and most specific matchup in the sport.
05
Compile video for each opponent
Located YouTube timestamps within 4-hour live streams, identified the correct piste, and compiled 3-4 match clips per opponent - focusing on bouts against left-handed or pistol grip fencers.
03 - Framework

The 4-profile system

In epee fencing, two variables define the tactical matchup: dominant hand (left or right) and grip type (pistol "crosse" or French "droite"). This creates four distinct profiles, each requiring a different tactical approach.

Auriane fences as a left-handed pistol grip (GC) fencer - the rarest profile among world-class competitors, which makes opponent experience against this profile particularly scarce and valuable to analyse.

GC - Gauche Crosse
Left-handed, Pistol grip
Auriane's profile. Rarest at elite level. Tactical priority for all opponent analysis.
GD - Gauche Droite
Left-handed, French grip
Secondary reference when GC matchup data was insufficient for a given opponent.
DC - Droite Crosse
Right-handed, Pistol grip
Mirror profile to GC. Some tactical overlap in grip mechanics.
DD - Droite Droite
Right-handed, French grip
Most common profile at elite level. Typically excluded from primary analysis.

05 - Individual Files

One file per opponent, round by round

For each of the 35 opponents, a dedicated Excel file tracked every match of every competition - opponent faced, their profile, score, and result. The goal: isolate all bouts where the opponent had faced a GC fencer, extracting the most tactically relevant reference data.

Each file was then filtered to highlight GC matchups specifically. Where GC data was scarce, GD bouts were added as secondary reference. Win/loss rates against each profile were calculated to identify tactical patterns.

06 - Video Work

Turning 4-hour livestreams into tactical clips

Competition fencing is broadcast as multi-hour livestreams covering 10+ simultaneous bouts across different pistes. Finding the right match meant cross-referencing competition schedules, piste assignments and timecodes - then manually scrubbing through the stream.

For each opponent with sufficient GC/GD matchup data, a video compilation of 3-4 bouts was assembled - specifically selecting bouts against the most tactically relevant profiles. These compilations gave Auriane and her coach direct visual reference for each opponent's patterns against a profile similar to her own.

The process was manual and time-intensive by nature: no fencing platform offers this level of matchup-filtered video access. This is what building a scouting system in an un-digitised sport looks like.

07 - Outcome

Data delivered, result achieved

The complete analysis - master dataset, individual opponent files, win/loss breakdowns by profile, and video compilations - was transmitted to Auriane and her coach ahead of the Games.

Paris 2024 Olympic Games Result
Auriane Mallo-Breton finished as double silver medallist at the Paris 2024 Olympics - in both the individual and team Women's Epee events.

This result is the product of 15+ years of elite training, physical preparation and technical mastery. The data work here was one small support tool in a much larger preparation effort - and is presented as exactly that. What it does demonstrate is that structured opponent analysis has a place in fencing, even when the infrastructure to support it barely exists yet.

08 - What This Shows
Analytical thinking applied to an unstructured domain
  • Ability to build a structured analytical framework from scratch, with no existing template or data infrastructure to rely on.
  • End-to-end project execution: problem definition, data sourcing, classification methodology, file building and video production - all self-directed.
  • Domain expertise matters: years of competitive fencing experience were essential to define the right framework, identify what data was relevant, and know what coaches actually need.
  • Resourcefulness with imperfect data: when clean data doesn't exist, the methodology adapts - secondary profiles, manual video review, cross-referencing multiple sources.
