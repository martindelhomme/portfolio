How it works

One file. Three fields. Full dashboard.

Upload your holdings file (CSV or Excel), enter your cash balance, total deposits and reporting currency below. The tool fetches live prices from Yahoo Finance — falling back to last close if the market is closed — converts everything to your chosen currency, and builds the dashboard entirely in your browser.

No data leaves your browser. Nothing is stored anywhere. Refresh to reset.
