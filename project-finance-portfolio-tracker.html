How it works

Two CSV files. Instant dashboard.

Inspired by institutional asset management reporting tools, this tracker was built to monitor a personal portfolio with the same rigour applied at work — allocation breakdowns, live PnL, sector and country exposure — without relying on any paid service or backend infrastructure.

Upload your holdings CSV and a settings file with your cash balance and total deposits. The tool fetches live prices and FX rates, converts everything to your chosen reporting currency, and builds the full dashboard in the browser.

No data leaves your browser. Nothing is stored. Refresh the page and it resets.
