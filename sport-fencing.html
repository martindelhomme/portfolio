Background

I started fencing at the age of 5, first in foil and later in épée. A member of Lyon Épée Métropole since the age of 10, I reached the international level in the Cadet and Junior categories. Among my best results are six medals at the French National Championships.

What I enjoy most about fencing is pushing myself to my limits and making the best possible decisions under intense pressure and stress.

I still train regularly, combining fencing with running and other sports. While competition is no longer my primary focus, I would like to return for tournaments from time to time and continue challenging myself.

The analytical side of fencing is explored in the Sport Analytics section, in particular the 2024 Paris Olympics Analysis, where data and video were used to support opponent analysis for a French epee fencer.
