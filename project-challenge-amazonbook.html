01 · Context & Motivation

Why this project started

The book was originally published in French and focuses on fencing technique and strategy. While an English translation had already been completed, it had never been published, making the content inaccessible to a large portion of the international fencing community. The missing piece was not the translation itself, but the formatting, adaptation, and publication process required to make the book available through Amazon KDP.

The project mattered to me because I was already familiar with the subject matter through my own fencing background, and because the English version had remained unpublished for years despite being ready. No one involved had the time or experience to handle the technical publishing process, so without someone taking ownership of it, the project would likely have remained unfinished.

"The translation already existed, the challenge was turning a completed manuscript into a published book that could actually reach an international audience."
02 · Objective

One clear goal

Deliver a publication-ready book in English, formatted to Amazon KDP and Kindle standards, listed and available for purchase on Amazon, built entirely without external help or prior publishing experience.

03 · Approach & Execution

How it was built

No prior experience in publishing, formatting, or the Amazon ecosystem. The project was broken down into discrete and learnable steps, each one a small problem to solve before moving to the next.

01
Manuscript & structure
The starting point was a single Word document containing the English translation, alongside the original French edition used as a reference. My objective was to preserve the structure and readability of the original work while adapting it to the requirements of both paperback and digital formats. I reviewed the manuscript chapter by chapter, reorganizing layouts, page breaks, headings, and navigation elements to create a consistent reading experience. Because the English edition was being published independently from the original French publisher, I also had flexibility to optimize certain elements specifically for Kindle and print readers rather than strictly replicating the original design.
02
KDP formatting standards
Since I had no prior experience with Amazon KDP, a significant part of the project involved learning the platform's technical requirements. This included understanding page dimensions, margins, headers and footers, table of contents generation, cover specifications, and the differences between paperback and ebook formatting. What surprised me most was that the actual publication process was relatively straightforward once the document was properly prepared. The most time-consuming part was ensuring that every chapter, section, and formatting element remained consistent across multiple review cycles and across both publication formats.
03
Cover design & metadata
Beyond the manuscript itself, I also redesigned and adapted the cover for the English edition using Canva and PowerPoint. The objective was not to replicate the original French cover exactly, but to create a version suitable for Amazon's publishing ecosystem while remaining consistent with the identity of the book. I also configured the publication details required by Amazon KDP, including book descriptions, categories, keywords, and product information to ensure the book could be properly listed and discovered by readers interested in fencing and sports strategy.
04
Review & publication
To validate the final product, I relied extensively on Kindle Previewer and repeated formatting reviews. The process involved several full passes through the manuscript to identify inconsistencies, verify navigation, and ensure compatibility across devices and print formats. I created chapter-by-chapter checklists to systematically review the content and avoid overlooking small formatting issues. After multiple iterations, both the paperback and ebook versions were successfully published on Amazon KDP without requiring major revisions during the platform's review process.
04 · Outcome

What happened

The project was successfully completed, and the English version of the book is now available on Amazon in both Kindle and paperback formats. I handled the formatting, cover adaptation, and KDP publication process from a translated Word manuscript to a finished product. The main lesson was that attention to detail during formatting and review matters far more than the publication step itself. The experience also gave me a repeatable process that can be reused for future editions. Final Product: https://www.amazon.ca/Spirit-%C3%89p%C3%A9e-Tactical-Mental-Approach-ebook/dp/B0DR7KQJC9

05 · What This Shows
Transferable value
  • Ability to learn quickly in an unfamiliar domain: publishing was a new process that required understanding technical constraints and adapting along the way.
  • End-to-end project ownership: taking a translated manuscript from draft format to a published product available on a global platform.
  • Attention to detail under constraints: ensuring consistency across ebook and paperback formats through multiple review cycles.
  • Long-term commitment: writing a full manuscript in a second language requires sustained discipline over weeks.
