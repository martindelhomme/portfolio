Background

I started running after the end of my international fencing career. Initially, it was a way to stay active while exploring new cities. Over time, it became a personal challenge, driven by the same analytical mindset I had developed through years of competitive sport: setting goals, measuring progress, and continuously looking for improvement.

After achieving my previous goal of running a marathon in under 3h40, I am now working toward new targets: Marathon: Sub 3h35, Half Marathon: Sub 1h35, 10K: Sub 44 minutes My current training focuses on balancing these objectives alongside a full-time career, using structured planning and consistent execution.
